eSIM
What is an eSIM and how is it different from a physical SIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM installed directly on your device, so there is no physical card to insert or replace. It works like a regular SIM, giving you access to Halo Telco network, calls and data.

The main advantage of eSIM is that you can activate it through the app without visiting a store, and it is ideal for devices without a physical SIM slot.

Which devices support eSIM?

Most smartphones released from 2019 onwards support eSIM, including:

  • iPhone XS and later
  • Samsung Galaxy S20 and later, plus Galaxy Z Fold/Flip series
  • Google Pixel 3 and later
  • Many recent Huawei, Oppo, Vivo and Xiaomi models

To check compatibility, open Settings > Cellular Network on your device and look for “Add eSIM” or “Add Cellular Plan”.

How do I get a Halo Telco eSIM?

You can request an eSIM directly through the Halo Telco app:

  • Download and sign in to the Halo Telco app
  • Choose your preferred Epik+ plan
  • Select eSIM as the SIM type during registration
  • Complete identity verification (e-KYC) in the app
  • An eSIM QR code will be generated for scanning
PopularHow do I activate an eSIM using the QR code?

Once your eSIM QR code is generated:

  • Open Settings > Cellular Network / Mobile Data > Add eSIM on your device
  • Scan the QR code provided in the Halo Telco app
  • Follow the on-screen instructions to complete installation
  • Allow 1–5 minutes for network activation
  • Restart the device if the network is not detected immediately

The eSIM QR code can only be scanned once — make sure you have a Wi-Fi connection during scanning.

Physical SIM
How do I activate a new physical SIM?

Insert the SIM into your device, download the Halo Telco app, register using the SIM number, and complete identity verification (e-KYC) as instructed in the app. Activation usually takes 5–30 minutes after successful verification.

Can I switch my physical SIM to an eSIM?

Yes. Your existing number and balance will be retained. You can request the switch through the Halo Telco app (Akaun > Tukar ke eSIM) or at a nearby Halo Telco partner store. Your old physical SIM will be deactivated once the new eSIM is successfully activated.

ImportantMy SIM is lost or damaged — how do I get a replacement?

Please visit any nearby Halo Telco partner store and bring your original identity card to request a replacement SIM. Your existing number will be retained. If your SIM was stolen or lost, we recommend temporarily blocking the account through the app first to prevent misuse.

Issues & Solutions
PopularWhy does my SIM or eSIM have no network?

Try these basic steps in order:

  • Turn Airplane Mode on for 10 seconds, then turn it off
  • Restart your device
  • Check APN settings using the guide in the Halo Telco app
  • Make sure your account balance is sufficient and your plan is still active

If the issue continues, contact our customer support team via WhatsApp for further assistance.

How do I move my number to a new device?

For a physical SIM, simply move the SIM to your new device. For eSIM, sign in to the Halo Telco app on your new device and select Transfer eSIM to This Device to generate a new QR code.
